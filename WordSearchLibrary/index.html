          How To Play

          The objective of a word search is to find all the listed words in the word grid. Words may be placed in any direction but must be in a straight line. When a word is found, mark the word by sliding over all the letters in the word. The word in the list will be automaticaly marked out.

          If you need a hint for a word, tap the word in the list and the first letter in the grid will be highlighted.
